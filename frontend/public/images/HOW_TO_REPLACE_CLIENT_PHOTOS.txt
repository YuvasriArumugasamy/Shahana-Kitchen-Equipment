========================================================================
  SHAHANA KITCHEN EQUIPMENT - CLIENT PHOTOS REPLACEMENT GUIDE
========================================================================

Follow these simple 3 steps to replace any website photo with your client's original photos:

STEP 1: Copy your Client Photos
-------------------------------
Save your client's photos into this folder:
frontend/public/images/

(Or create a subfolder like frontend/public/images/client/ and paste them inside)


STEP 2: Open the Configuration File
-----------------------------------
Open this file in your editor:
[galleryData.js](file:///c:/Users/HP/OneDrive/Attachments/OneDrive/Desktop/files/shahana%20kitchen%20eqipment/frontend/src/data/galleryData.js)


STEP 3: Update the Image Name
-----------------------------
In galleryData.js, change the `img` line to your new photo name.

Example:
Change:
    img: "/images/instant wet grinder.png"

To:
    img: "/images/my_client_wet_grinder.jpg"


That's it! Save the file and refresh your browser. The website will immediately show the client's original photos.
